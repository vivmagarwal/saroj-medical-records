## 9. Questions the family would like to put to Dr. Purushottam

These are framed as questions, not opinions — they are the points where the record seems to bear on the decision.

1. **Which of the two drugs is actually prolonging her QT?** Amiodarone is a class III agent whose defining effect is QT prolongation. 2. **How much of the "prolonged QTc" is a Bazett artefact?** Her raw QT is 377–440 ms at heart rates of 84–94 bpm, and all five July traces are Bazett-corrected. On Fridericia the last two read 462 and 450 ms. Is that worth confirming before the regimen is changed?

Is the QT partly a treatable electrolyte problem rather than purely a drug-class problem?
4. **Does the clean 72-hour Holter change the risk calculus?** No VT, no pauses, 0.1 % ectopic burden, and her symptomatic episode occurred in sinus rhythm. Does she still need two antiarrhythmics at all — or is de-escalation of one of them reasonable?
